Siddha Therapies & Wellness

Traditional therapies designed for holistic health and better quality of life.

Thokkanam

It is a Siddha external therapy and one of the most adopted therapies used in olden days by native healers to promote mental and physical fitness.

Benefits

  • Reduces body aches and pain
  • Helps manage spondylosis
  • Improves muscle consistency
  • Promotes sound sleep
  • Improves skin texture

Varmam

It is a unique external therapy given by the Siddhars to treat multiple ailments and has a broad science behind it.

Benefits

  • Improves circulation
  • Relieves tension and stress
  • Improves sleep
  • Helps musculoskeletal conditions
  • Supports relaxation

Dorn Therapy

Dorn Therapy originated in Germany and has similarities with Varmam and acupressure techniques. It is primarily used for pain management and spine alignment.

Benefits

  • Supports joint alignment
  • Pain management
  • Frozen shoulder support
  • Stiff neck support
  • Headache and migraine support

Diet Counseling

According to the quote "Food is thy Medicine and Medicine is thy Food", we provide diet counseling according to the state and nature of disease.

Benefits

  • Personalized diet guidance
  • Supports healthy lifestyle
  • Helps manage lifestyle-related conditions
  • Supports preventive care
